## Operational evidence to provide

- **Security/Records Owner:** approved class definitions, permitted evidence scope, restrictions, retention and hold rules, disposal authority, and approval reference.
- **Academic Owner:** evidence classes needed for the approved instrument and workflow, including any content that must be excluded or redacted.
- **School IT:** implementation of role access, backup/log retention and holds, protected copy handling, and disposal procedure with evidence references.
- **Project Owner / recorder:** record unresolved inputs, named follow-up owner, checkpoint, and approval evidence in the [workshop tracker](PILOT_READINESS_WORKSHOP_TRACKER.md). Keep D12 Pending Decision until the required authorities approve it.

No automatic purge or records deletion is authorized by this form. Any such mechanism needs a separate approved design covering historical references, holds, backups, and auditability.
